Conveyor belt analytics

AI-powered waste analytics.

Camera-based analytics for sorting and recycling plants. Material types, item counts and contamination on the conveyor belt, measured continuously.

Get in touch →

What it measures.

The belt is recorded continuously and every item on it is detected and tracked. The result is a running record of what passes through the line.

01

Composition

The share of each material type on the belt.

02

Item count

The number of items in each category.

03

Contamination

Unwanted material that ends up in a stream.

04

Change over time

How the belt content shifts by hour, shift and day.

How it works.

Each installation is planned around the plant: belt width, material stream and what the plant wants to track.

Step 1

Camera above the belt

A camera and a small computer are mounted over an existing conveyor. The line itself does not change.

Step 2

Vision model

The model detects each item, assigns it to a category and follows it along the belt so it is counted once.

Step 3

Analytics for the plant

Counts, composition and trends are delivered to the plant team as regular reports.

Who it is for.

Any plant that moves waste or recovered material on a conveyor belt.

Sorting

Packaging waste sorting plants

Visibility into incoming material and into what each line actually separates.

Recycling

Recycling plants

Plastic, paper, metal, construction and hazardous waste recyclers checking the quality of the material they receive and ship.
